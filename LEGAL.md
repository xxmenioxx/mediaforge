# Legal and Responsible Use

MVForge is a self-hosted media processing and management tool. It analyzes and
transforms media files supplied by the user and can produce converted,
restored, validated, or republished derivatives.

## User responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the rights and permissions
required to access, copy, process, modify, translate, publish, or distribute
the media they use with MVForge.

Copyright, contract, technological-protection-measure, privacy, and other laws
vary by jurisdiction. The presence of a feature in MVForge does not itself
grant permission to use third-party content.

## Product boundaries

MVForge does not provide copyrighted media and is not intended to be a media
acquisition service.

The project does not implement or bundle features whose primary purpose is to
circumvent access controls or digital rights management (DRM), including:

- AACS or BD+ circumvention;
- CSS circumvention;
- Widevine or similar DRM bypass;
- retrieval or distribution of decryption keys;
- DRM stripping; or
- bypassing technological protection measures.

MVForge may process media that is already accessible to the user. Tools or
workflows used before an asset reaches MVForge remain outside MVForge's scope.
